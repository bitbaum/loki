# UX pass — November 2026

**created_date:** 2026-11-12
**last_modified_date:** 2026-11-12
**last_modified_summary:** First version: what was looked at and how, what changed with the measurement behind each change, and what is next with its plan.

The owner asked for a pass over user experience and interface (the paths
people take, hierarchy, graphic design, consistency, copy, states,
accessibility) held to the bar of the best product teams, using the shared
packages and changing them where they fall short. This file records what was
measured, what changed and what is next. The bar itself is not restated here:
it is [`design-reset-2026-10.md`](design-reset-2026-10.md) and fleet
`AGENTS.md` ("No dead ends", "One shape per kind", "The experience belongs to
the package").

## How it was looked at

- Every public page and every signed-in page, at 390×844 and 1440×900, dark and
  light, in a real browser, signed in to two local accounts: the dense fixture
  (`pnpm run seed:showcase`, 42 projects) and the demo tenant (three projects,
  first-run and empty states).
- What a still cannot show was driven: menus, the phone drawer, keyboard focus,
  a typed sentence walked from the homepage through sign-up and onboarding with
  a newly created account.
- The homepage and the public header, menu and footer had a full written audit
  (13 findings, each with a measurement). The signed-in surfaces were reviewed
  from the captures and by walking them; they did not get the same written
  audit, and "What is next" says so.

## What changed, and the measurement behind it

### The way in

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The headline says "Say what you want" and the only action under it opened a four-field "Create an account" form that never mentioned a project. | The homepage's one action is the shared composer (`HeroComposer`). What a signed-out visitor says is kept in the tab (`lib/first-words`), shown back on sign-up, and is in the new-project form when they arrive. |
| Onboarding asked a new person for a project name, a folder and a GitHub address. | With a sentence carried, that form is skipped (the same path as its own "Skip for now") and the sentence becomes the project's brief. |
| The microphone on `/change` recorded a whole take and then failed for a signed-out visitor: the transcriber answers 401 without a session. | Signed out, the box uses the browser's own recogniser (`VISITOR_VOICE`); where there is none, no microphone is drawn. |

Walked with a new account at 390px: homepage → sign-up (sentence shown) →
"You're ready to build" → the new-project form with the sentence in "What do
you want to build?".

### The homepage

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Length at 390×844 | 10.66 screens | 7.48 screens |
| Length at 1440×900 | 6.6 screens | 5.11 screens |
| Words above the footer (visible text, 390px) | about 1,300 | about 780 |

| Labels for the one sign-up destination | 4 ("Get started", "Get started — free", "Start a project", "Create an account") | 1 ("Start a project") |

- The problem list is three rows on the homepage with one link to all twelve on
  `/why` (it was four of the page's ten phone screens).
- "In the cloud, or on your own computer" is one sentence and two links inside
  "You stay in charge", not a section of its own.
- The closing block is the shared `FinalCta`, not a hand-copied variant.
- With no showcased project, the hero's evidence is one line of real counts
  with zeros left out, not a box around "0 agents running".

### The public header, menu and footer

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The header was `position: sticky` and scrolled away: its top measured −2000px after a 2000px scroll, because `.ui-public-surface` was `overflow: hidden` and so became the sticky container. | `overflow-x: clip`. The header's top measures 0 after the same scroll, on every public page. |
| Opening the phone menu left keyboard focus on the page behind it: 40 Tab presses reached none of the menu's links. | Focus enters the menu, 45 Tab presses stay inside it, Escape hands focus back to the menu button (`useDialogFocus`). |
| Passing the pointer over "Product" dropped a 720px panel over the headline and left it there. | The menu opens on a press or Enter only; two groups in two columns; it no longer claims `role="menu"`. |
| Between 1024px and 1280px the whole navigation and "Sign in" sat behind a menu button beside about 700px of empty bar. | The navigation, the theme control and "Sign in" show from 1120px. |
| The keyboard focus ring was 1px at half alpha, 2.0:1 against the page. | 2px in the text colour with a 2px offset: on the public surface, and as the app-wide fallback wherever a component draws none of its own. |
| No skip link. | "Skip to content" is the first Tab stop on every public page. |
| Five footer groups: one alone under a 2×2 on a phone, an empty cell on a desk. | Four groups: 2×2 on a phone, one row of four from 1024px. The studio-sites disclosure carries a chevron. |

### Corners nobody had drawn

- An unknown address outside the app drew the framework's bare "404 | This page
  could not be found." with no way back. It is now the public header, one line
  and one button (`src/app/not-found.tsx`).
- The in-app not-found page's button said "Back to Today" and went to Loki. One
  line, one button that names where it goes.
- The in-app error page printed the raw error text. One sentence and "Try
  again"; the text still goes to the debug log.
- The loading placeholder drew a title, a sentence and four cards, a layout no
  page has had since the design reset. It is rows, on the list's own classes.
- The image a shared link shows leads with the homepage's sentence.

### Inside the app

- **Dates no longer hydrate twice.** `APP_LOCALE` is a server variable, so a
  client component formatting a date used the default locale while the server
  used the configured one. With `APP_LOCALE=de-CH` that was a React hydration
  error on `/approvals`. `next.config.ts` now hands the same value to the
  browser as `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_LOCALE`.
- **Home's running rows lead with the live step.** A phone row holds about
  thirty characters; every row read "Next best step · Editing src/app/pa…".
- **Workbench rows on a phone** were each cut before the words that said what
  the row is for. All three lines now fit 390px.
- **Terminal, without cloud access:** "Cloud runner is private for this
  account. Use This computer after connecting Fleet Runner." became "The cloud
  computer is for invited accounts during the beta. Your own computer works
  too, with the free Fleet Runner app."
- **First-run Home:** "Connect a runner" is "Connect your computer"; "Widget
  not on live site" is "Loki button not on the live site"; the offline row's
  grey line fits a phone.

### Text boxes get the microphone (the experience standard)

`node <fleet>/scripts/ci/experience-audit.mjs --dir .`

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Hand-made text areas with no microphone | 34 | 1 |
| Ceiling in `package-plan.json` | none (report only) | set to today's counts, so it can only fall |

Thirty-two files now use `<TextArea>` (`src/components/ui/text-area.tsx`),
Loki's one adapter over `@bitbaum/chatkit`'s `DictationField`, drawn to match
`ui-input`. One file is exempt with its reason on its first line (the review
paths box takes URL paths).

It took two steps. Sixteen fitted the field as it was (chatkit 0.22.1 → 0.25.0).
The other sixteen needed things a form's text area has and the shared field
did not pass through: `autoFocus`, `onKeyDown`, a submit key, a blur that
saves, `name`, `spellCheck`. Those were added to the package, with tests and
an independent review, in
[bitbaum/chatkit#85](https://github.com/bitbaum/chatkit/pull/85) (0.27.0), and
the files moved onto it: Cmd/Ctrl+Enter saves where Cmd+Enter did, Escape
still cancels, Enter still sends an interview answer, an inline editor still
saves when focus leaves it and no longer closes when its microphone is
pressed. Reviewing the first sixteen found that a dictated take could pass a
field's `maxLength`; that is fixed in
[bitbaum/chatkit#87](https://github.com/bitbaum/chatkit/pull/87) (0.28.1),
which Loki now runs.

The one left is `src/components/control/prompt-input.tsx`: a prompt box with
its own attachments, paste-to-attach and microphone. It is a composer, not a
form field, so its way onto the package is the shared `Composer`, not
`<TextArea>`.

### Measured and left alone

Text contrast: every text tier on every surface, both themes, computed from the
tokens. The lowest is 4.62:1 (`--text-muted` on `--surface-overlay`, light);
all pass the 4.5:1 floor. The note in `scripts/test/contrast-audit.mjs` that
`--text-tertiary` is below it on dark is out of date.

## What is next, with its plan

1. **The last hand-made text box**, `control/prompt-input.tsx`, onto the
   shared `Composer` (see above), and the ceiling in `package-plan.json` to
   zero.
2. **The public header, menu and footer onto `@bitbaum/sitekit`.** They are
   Loki's own markup (`PublicNav`, `PublicFooter`, `PublicSurface`). sitekit's
   `SiteMasthead` (application appearance) cannot yet express two things this
   header needs: a nav entry that leaves the site, and one action kept beside
   the menu button on a phone. Extend sitekit with those two, release, then
   adopt. The app's `Sidebar` and `MobileNav` (the audit's `localChrome=2`) need
   a sidebar and a tab-bar item that is a button ("More"), which sitekit does
   not have either.
3. **A written audit of the signed-in surfaces**, one surface at a time, with
   the same evidence standard the homepage got: Loki chat, Home, Projects and
   the project page, Terminal, the work queues, Settings and the private zone,
   and a measured pass over type scale, spacing and motion. Seen in this pass
   and not changed: Approvals mixes two row shapes and draws one "Approve" per
   row in the primary style; the project page opens on a boxed card under a
   kicker; "runner" is still the app's word in many places where the person's
   word is "computer"; the new-project form explains itself under its title and
   suggests an unrelated example name.
4. **A sentence a phone cannot finish.** Home's "Needs you" rows put the
   outcome and the next step on one line ("The last run only got part of the
   way — next: …"); at 390px about a third of it shows, and the repo's own
   responsive audit warns about it. It needs a decision on the row (two lines
   for an alert, or the next step on the page the row opens), not a shorter
   sentence.
5. **Sign-up itself.** Four fields and a second password box stand between a
   sentence and a project. Accounts are `@bitbaum/accountkit`'s; a shorter form
   is a change there.

## How to check a change like this

```bash
pnpm run seed:showcase                       # the dense local account
pnpm run dev
BASE=http://localhost:3000 AUDIT_EMAIL=owner@loki.local \
  AUDIT_PASSWORD=showcase-owner node scripts/audit-pages.mjs .tmp/audit
```

`scripts/test/responsive-audit.mjs` (`pnpm run audit:responsive`) is the
permanent check for sideways scroll, cut words and touch targets. The
before/after pictures for this pass are in the pull request.
