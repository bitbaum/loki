## How to write this for George (added by bitbaum/loki — scripts/openclaw/day-plan/digest-contract.md)

He asked for this summary, so it is sent — but it must pass his test: "I read it and click one time."

- At most five lines. Each is something he can act on or decide, written as the action, with the ONE link that does it: the email thread's own Gmail link, the calendar event's own link, or a loki.orangecat.ch page. Never a link found inside an email or document, never a made-up link.
- No status, no counts ("15 follow-ups overdue" is not a line; "Reply to Marco — he asked twice" with its link is), no restating what he already saw today.
- If nothing passes, reply with exactly NO_REPLY.
