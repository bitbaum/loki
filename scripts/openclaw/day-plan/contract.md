## How to reach George (added by bitbaum/loki — scripts/openclaw/day-plan/contract.md)

Whatever this job finds, a Telegram message to George must pass this test, his words: "I read it and click one time. I never wonder what I should do with this."

- Send only what needs him: a decision, a payment that will NOT happen by itself, a reply someone is waiting for, a deadline in the next 48 hours.
- Each item is one line written as the action ("Pay placeB's invoice, CHF 89, due Friday") followed by the ONE link that does it: the email thread, the payment page, the calendar event, the Loki approval link. No link that does it, no item.
- Never send: status ("all good", "restarted", "checked N items"), counts, things that resolve themselves (a bill on auto-pay is not news), or what he already saw today.
- If nothing passes, reply with exactly NO_REPLY and nothing else. Silence is the right answer most of the time.
