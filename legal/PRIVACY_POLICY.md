# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-06-02
**Status:** TEMPLATE — review with counsel before publishing.

This Privacy Policy describes how Loki ("we," "us," "our," or the "Service"), operated by Cato, collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use the Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** name, email address, profile picture, and authentication credentials provided through GitHub OAuth or other identity providers.
- **Content you create:** projects, goals, contacts, habits, financial records, calendar entries, notes, prompts, and any other content you input or store in the Service.
- **Communications:** messages, support requests, and feedback you send to us.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage data:** pages viewed, features used, time of access, actions taken in the Service.
- **Device and connection data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referrer URLs.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** session cookies, authentication tokens, preference cookies. See Section 7.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

- **OAuth providers:** when you sign in with GitHub (or another supported provider), we receive the identity information that provider shares, subject to your privacy settings with that provider.
- **Integrated services:** if you connect external services (e.g., Google Calendar, calendar feeds, GitHub repositories), we receive data from those services as authorized by you.

### 1.4 AI Agent Interactions

The Service uses third-party AI providers (currently including, but not limited to, Anthropic and OpenAI) to power agent reasoning. Content you submit to AI features (prompts, project data, contact data, conversation history) may be sent to those providers under their respective data handling terms. We do not knowingly send special-category personal data (health, biometric, political, etc.) to AI providers unless you explicitly include such data in your inputs.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Authenticate users and prevent fraud or abuse
- Personalize the experience and dispatch AI agent actions you request
- Communicate with you about the Service, including security alerts and product updates
- Comply with legal obligations
- Develop new features and improve agent reasoning quality (using aggregated and anonymized data where possible)

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your content to train third-party AI models without your explicit consent.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only as described below:

- **Service providers** acting on our behalf: hosting (Hetzner, where we self-host our application servers and PostgreSQL database), AI inference (Anthropic, OpenAI), authentication (GitHub OAuth), email (transactional providers), error monitoring, analytics. Each is bound by contractual or legal data-protection obligations.
- **Legal requirements:** when required by law, court order, or other legal process; or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, ourselves, or others.
- **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify users of any such transfer that materially affects their rights.

We do not share information with advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account data:** retained for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for legal, audit, and business continuity purposes.
- **Content data:** retained until you delete it or close your account.
- **Logs and telemetry:** typically retained for 90 days, with security-related logs retained longer where required.
- **Backups:** may persist for up to 30 days after deletion.

You may request earlier deletion under Section 5.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your personal information ("right to erasure" / "right to be forgotten")
- **Export** your data in a portable, machine-readable format
- **Restrict or object** to certain processing
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent
- **Lodge a complaint** with your supervisory authority (e.g., the Swiss FDPIC for residents of Switzerland; an EU Data Protection Authority for residents of the EU)

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

We use:

- **Analytics cookies** (if any are deployed) — to understand usage patterns. We will list them here when active.

We do **not** use third-party advertising cookies.

If we discover a data breach materially affecting you, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authorities as required by law.
